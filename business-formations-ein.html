What this covers

  • LLC and corporation formation document preparation and filing
  • EIN applications
  • S-corporation election support (Form 2553 preparation)
  • Startup tax setup and first-year compliance calendar
  • General tax information about entity choice

Who this is for

New business owners forming an LLC or corporation, and existing owners formalizing a business that has been running as a sole proprietorship.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need. Share your service needs, relevant tax years, states, timeline and contact preference through the intake.
  2. Confirm scope and next steps. We review your request, confirm the scope in writing and quote the fee before work begins.
  3. Organize the documents. We provide the secure document-collection process and identify missing records or cleanup needs.
  4. Engagement and preparation. Work proceeds within the agreed, signed scope — preparation, bookkeeping, payroll or business support — with review and filing authorization where applicable.
  5. Filing, completion and follow-up. A return is reported as filed only with verified filing acknowledgment. Then we plan your ongoing support: recurring bookkeeping, payroll and tax-readiness.

Start with the intake — never with sensitive documents

Use the initial intake for contact details and a description of your needs. Do not submit Social Security numbers, full EINs, dates of birth, account numbers, login credentials, tax returns or payroll files. After your request is reviewed, we provide the secure document-collection process for anything sensitive.

Service & credential clarity

Michael Fusco holds a PTIN and EFIN. Prestige Tax Group, Inc. does not hold itself out as a CPA firm, enrolled-agent practice or law firm. Work requiring CPA, EA or attorney credentials is coordinated with the appropriate qualified professional.

Prestige preparers are PTIN-registered preparers, and the firm is an IRS-authorized e-file provider. Preparation, bookkeeping and payroll support are provided within that authority; matters outside it are referred or coordinated as described above.

Questions

Should I be an LLC or an S-corporation?

We can walk through the general tax considerations with you. The legal side of entity choice — liability, contracts, operating agreements — is attorney work, and we refer that part rather than advise on it.

Do you write operating agreements or contracts?

No. Document preparation here means formation and tax filings. Legal documents are prepared by your attorney.
