About the firm

Prestige Tax Group, Inc. is a virtual tax firm offering tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll support, business formations and QuickBooks services. It is powered by Legacy Wise Advisors, Inc. and is part of Legacy Wise Enterprises — the contracting entity for every Prestige engagement is Prestige Tax Group, Inc. itself.

Work that requires a credential Prestige does not hold — attest work, appeals and collections representation, legal documents — is coordinated with or referred to the appropriate qualified professional, and that line is stated plainly before any engagement begins.

Service & credential clarity

Michael Fusco holds a PTIN and EFIN. Prestige Tax Group, Inc. does not hold itself out as a CPA firm, enrolled-agent practice or law firm. Work requiring CPA, EA or attorney credentials is coordinated with the appropriate qualified professional.

Prestige preparers are PTIN-registered preparers, and the firm is an IRS-authorized e-file provider. Preparation, bookkeeping and payroll support are provided within that authority; matters outside it are referred or coordinated as described above.
